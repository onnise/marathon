About the Race

More Than Just a Race

The City Marathon is an annual running event that brings together athletes and beginners from around the world. Whether you're chasing a personal best or crossing your first finish line — there's a distance for everyone.

Set through the heart of the city, the course takes you past iconic landmarks, cheering crowds, and live music stations every kilometre.

  • ✓ Open to all fitness levels
  • ✓ Medical support along the route
  • ✓ Timing chip & official certificate
  • ✓ Finisher medal & race kit
