About the Race

More Than Just a Race

The Bikfaya 5K Eco Race is a LAF-certified, eco-friendly running event set against the stunning mountain scenery of Bikfaya. Whether you're a competitive runner chasing a personal best or a family out for a fun morning together — there's a race for everyone.

Starting and finishing at Bikfaya Square, the course winds through the scenic streets of this beautiful mountain town, with spectators cheering every step of the way.

  • ✓ LAF certified & approved
  • ✓ Eco-friendly event ♻️
  • ✓ Cash prizes + trophies & medals
  • ✓ Post-race breakfast for all finishers

Race Day

Sunday, September 20, 2026

All events start and finish at Bikfaya Square. Timings are tentative and subject to minor change.

7:00 AM

Assembly

Arrive at Bikfaya Square (start line near Alfa Store), collect your race kit, warm up, and get ready.

8:00 AM

5K Competitive Race 🏁

Official LAF-certified 5K race. Chip-timed with category rankings for men and women.

9:00 AM

2K Fun Run 🎉

Open to everyone — all ages, families, and beginners. No competition, all fun.

10:00 AM

Race Closes

All participants should have crossed the finish line. Medical and support teams on standby throughout.

11:00 AM

Awards & Breakfast 🥐

Prize ceremony for category and overall winners. Followed by manakish, fresh fruits, hot & cold beverages for all.

FAQ

Common Questions

The official chip-timed results are available on the Results page. You can search by name, filter by gender or age category, and sort by finish time or overall place.

Gun time is measured from the starting signal (gun) to the moment you cross the finish line. Chip time (net time) starts when you personally cross the start mat — it's your true personal running time. Category rankings are based on chip time.

The 5K is a certified competitive race with chip timing, official results, category rankings and cash prizes. The 2K is a non-competitive fun run open to all ages and families — no pressure, all fun.
